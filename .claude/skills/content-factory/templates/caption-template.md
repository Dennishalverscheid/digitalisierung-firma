# Instagram Caption — Template

Carousel captions live or die in the first 125 characters (the "see more" cutoff on mobile). Front-load the value, then expand.

## Shape

```
[HOOK — 1 line, ≤125 chars, restates slide 1 differently]

[CONTEXT — 1–2 short paragraphs. Pulls from slides 2 + 3 speaker notes.]

[USE CASE — 1 paragraph. Pulls from slide 4 speaker note. Concrete.]

[POV — 1 paragraph. Pulls from slides 5–6. The "so what."]

[CTA — 1 line. Matches slide 7 exactly so the swipe → caption flow is consistent.]

[CREDIT — 1 line. "Source: huggingface.co/<model>" — never bury the source.]

[HASHTAGS — block of 8–15, mixed in size. No #love #instagood — only niche-relevant tags.]
```

## Hard rules

- ≤2200 chars total (Instagram limit).
- No emojis unless brand voice explicitly uses them.
- No "link in bio" unless there's a link in bio.
- Hashtags go at the BOTTOM, not inline — keeps the reading flow clean.
- Source link / model name appears in plain text, not as a fake hyperlink.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Opening with "🚨 BREAKING:" — looks like spam.
- Ending with "thoughts?" — generates 0 comments because there's no specific thing to react to.
- Padding the caption to look longer. Short captions outperform padded ones.
- Using "we" when you mean the brand — it's distancing. Either name the brand or speak in the second person.
